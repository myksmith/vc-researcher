Ideally a named principal, CIO, or family-office manager with authority to approve direct startup deals.

## Disqualifiers (Poor Fit)

- Invests only as LP in VC funds (fund-of-funds only) with no direct startup deals on record
- Portfolio restricted to real estate, public equities, or private equity buyouts
- No disclosed or verifiable direct tech startup investments in the last 5 years
- Entirely opaque structure with no named decision-maker or contact point
- No investment activity of any kind in the last 3 years

## Warm Introduction Pathways

Family offices rely almost exclusively on trusted introductions. Research should surface:

- Shared LP relationships
- Operating-company ties that overlap with your network or customer base
- Named advisors, board members, or intermediaries linked to the family office
- Founder network connections
